DeepSeek-V3 decode on an NVL72 rack: GB200 vs GB300, BF16 vs FP4

3 runs side by side

First 4 of 61 layers (3 dense, 1 MoE), expert parallel over all 72 GPUs, 64 sequences per GPU at 4k context, one token each. Memory is checked for the whole model.

Decode GEMMs are weight-bandwidth bound, so FP4 helps through bytes moved, not through the 3x higher FP4 tensor peak; attention and the all-to-alls do not change.
